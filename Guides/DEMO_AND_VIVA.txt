SHORT VIVA ANSWERS
Why .test? A reserved testing namespace; .local conflicts with multicast DNS.
Does DNS connect to the application? No. It returns an address; TCP then connects.
Why can ping pass while DNS fails? ICMP to an IP does not require name resolution.
What identifies a connection? Source/destination IPs and ports plus transport protocol.
Why three TCP packets? SYN, SYN-ACK and ACK establish sequence state in both directions.
Why Ack=1 initially? SYN consumes one position in TCP sequence space.
What is an ephemeral port? A temporary client-side port, here 56700 for the captured HTTPS flow.
Where does TLS end? At nginx; the backend hop uses plaintext HTTP on the LAN.
What does certificate validation check? Trusted certificate path/explicit trust,
validity dates and matching hostname; possession of the private key is established
through the handshake. A self-signed cert needs explicit local trust.
Why no -k? It bypasses certificate verification and would not prove trusted HTTPS.
Why TLS1.2 in the capture? It exposes the certificate handshake message for teaching.
What is SNI? The hostname sent in ClientHello so the server can choose a TLS configuration.
What does 304 mean? The selected representation matches the validator; reuse cached content.
Is curl automatically caching? Not in these tests. If-None-Match was explicitly supplied.
Why same ETag on A/B? Both serve identical /cache bytes and hash those bytes.
502 versus 504? A bad upstream exchange/connect refusal may produce 502;
an upstream timeout produces 504. Our retry chain ended in a timeout.
Active or passive checks? nginx used passive max_fails/fail_timeout and request retries.
HTTP1.1 versus HTTP2? HTTP2 multiplexes streams over one TCP connection;
HTTP1.1 uses textual message framing. The nginx backend hop was HTTP1.1.
HTTP3? Uses QUIC over UDP; explanation only, not implemented here.
Cloud analogy? Managed DNS, an application load balancer, and application instances.
Email analogy? SMTP submits/transfers mail; IMAP/POP retrieve it. No email service built.
